How to prepare your Google Doc ↗

1. Keep the original PDFs untouched. Upload the originals to ChatGPT and ask it to extract each file's PDF metadata directly, not to guess from its content or file name. If only screenshots are available, mark the result as incomplete.

2. Paste the result into one Google Doc. Use a separate block starting with File: for every PDF. Preserve the exact filename, extension, timestamps and time zones. Never merge multiple PDFs into one block.

3. After editing in Affinity, keep the PDF filenames unchanged. Share the Google Doc as Anyone with the link · Viewer, load it here, then review the matches before processing.

Accuracy note: A Google Doc extracted by AI is a reference, not a full copy of the source PDF's metadata. Screenshots cannot prove all Info, XMP and structural properties. Matching a filename does not prove the final PDF equals the original; compare the original and final in your PDF inspector before relying on them.

Restore policyMetaMorph restores Created, Modified, Application, PDF Producer, and clears Title only when the source record explicitly says it was blank. Other fields in the Doc are ignored.
ImportantUse the regular share URL containing /document/d/…/edit. Published /d/e/…/pub links are not supported.
